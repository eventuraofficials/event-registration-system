Questions, answered

Everything you need to feel ready.

Can I upload an existing Excel guest list?

Yes. Upload an Excel or CSV list, review valid rows and duplicates, then confirm the import.

Can guests register without an account?

Yes. Guests open the event link, complete the form, and receive their QR ticket without creating an account.

What happens if the camera cannot scan?

Staff can search by name, company, email, contact number, or guest code. Manual entry is always available.

Can I see attendance during the event?

Yes. The dashboard and check-in console show updated registration and attendance counts.
